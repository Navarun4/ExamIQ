Model note: step-by-step numerical integration (5 ms steps) with g = 9.8 m/s² and a quadratic drag term, acceleration = −k·|v|·v. Mass is folded into k, which is a teaching parameter, not a calibrated real-world coefficient. With k = 0 the result agrees with R = u²·sin 2θ / g to better than a millimetre. With k > 0 there is no simple formula; checked against a high-precision reference, the range is within about 1% (at most 0.3 m) across all slider settings, typically under 0.1% at k = 0.010. View: oblique (cabinet) projection. Distances along the front edge and heights match the scale bar; the receding direction is drawn at half scale, so read distances from the labels, not by eye.
