Inside Projectile Motion

What you'll actually do.

The lesson runs as one connected loop. Recall unlocks only after you have experimented in the lab.

  1. PredictPick the launch angle you think gives the greatest range, before any launch.
  2. ObserveLaunch at least two angles and compare their landing points, even if your guess was wrong.
  3. Spatial LabChange launch speed, angle, aim direction and air resistance and watch the 3-D trajectory respond.
  4. RecallAnswer a question about the vertical motion at the highest point, with feedback.
  5. TransferA bonus challenge applies the same principle to a new situation.
